Your money stays on your phone.

Kassa is a private money tracker for Android. It has no internet permission, no account, no ads and no analytics. It can't send your data anywhere, because it isn't allowed to connect.

Coming soon to Google PlayRead the privacy policy

Android only for now. An iPhone app is planned.

No INTERNET permission. Android apps without it cannot open network connections. Kassa's build removes it on purpose, and the check that stops a release if it ever appears is part of every build.

What it does

Track everything in one place

Accounts, cards, loans, people you split with, and investments. See what you own and what you owe, with assets, liabilities and net worth.

Loans that make sense

Add a mortgage or car loan with its terms. Each payment is split into principal, interest and escrow, and you can change the rate or terms at any point.

Hide balances in one tap

An eye button hides every amount on every screen, for when someone is looking over your shoulder.

Move in from other apps

Import from Money Manager or Cashew, and check each account's balance against the source.

Reminders without a network

Bills, card payments and loan payments wait for you on the day they are due. Nothing is paid or added on its own.

Your data, your folder

Backups go only to the folder you choose. Lock the app with your fingerprint or screen lock.

Why offline

Most money apps ask you to link your bank and trust a company with the result. Kassa takes the other route. You enter what you want to track, it stays encrypted on your phone, and nobody else can see it, including me.

The trade-off is real: Kassa can't pull in transactions from your bank for you. If automatic bank sync matters most to you, another app will suit you better.
